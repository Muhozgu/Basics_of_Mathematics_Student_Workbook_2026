# Solution 6

A square matrix is singular exactly when its determinant is zero. Thus

$$
\det A(t)
=
\begin{vmatrix}
t & 2 \\
3 & t-1
\end{vmatrix}
=t(t-1)-2(3)
=t^2-t-6
=(t-3)(t+2).
$$

Setting the determinant equal to zero gives

$$
(t-3)(t+2)=0,
$$

so the matrix is singular for

$$
\boxed{t=3 \text{ or } t=-2}.
$$
