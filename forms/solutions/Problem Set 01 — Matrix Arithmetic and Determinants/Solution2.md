# Solution 2

Scalar multiplication is performed entrywise:

$$
2A =
\begin{pmatrix}
4 & 2 \\
6 & 8
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
3B =
\begin{pmatrix}
3 & 6 \\
-6 & 0
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Subtracting corresponding entries gives

$$
2A - 3B =
\begin{pmatrix}
4-3 & 2-6 \\
6-(-6) & 8-0
\end{pmatrix}
=
\boxed{\begin{pmatrix}
1 & -4 \\
12 & 8
\end{pmatrix}}.
$$
