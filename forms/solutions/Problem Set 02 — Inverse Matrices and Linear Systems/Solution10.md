# Solution 10

Let

- $s$ = number of standard tickets,
- $t$ = number of student tickets.

The problem gives the system

$$
\begin{cases}s+t=30\\12s+8t=304\end{cases}.
$$

From the first equation,

$$
s=30-t.
$$

Substitute into the second equation:

$$
12(30-t)+8t=304
$$

$$
360-12t+8t=304
$$

$$
-4t=-56\quad\Rightarrow\quad t=14.
$$

Then

$$
s=30-14=16.
$$

So the meaningful solution is

$$
\boxed{s=16,\text{ }t=14}.
$$

Check:

$$
16+14=30,\qquad 12(16)+8(14)=192+112=304.
$$

Both values are nonnegative integers, so the answer makes sense in the context of the problem.
