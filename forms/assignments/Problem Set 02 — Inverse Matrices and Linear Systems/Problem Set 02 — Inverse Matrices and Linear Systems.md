# Problem Set 02 — Inverse Matrices and Linear Systems

## Section A: Inverse matrices and matrix methods

### Problem 1
Find $A^{-1}$ for the matrix

$$
A = \begin{pmatrix}
3 & 1 \\
2 & 1
\end{pmatrix},
$$
and verify that

$$
AA^{-1} = I.
$$

### Problem 2
Find the inverse of the matrix

$$
A = \begin{pmatrix}
1 & 1 & 0 \\
0 & 1 & 1 \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Show the row operations or solve $AX = I$.

### Problem 3
Solve the system

$$
\begin{cases}
2x + y = 7 \\
x - y = 2
\end{cases}
$$

using Cramer's rule. Check the condition for using the rule.

## Section B: Gaussian elimination and Cramer’s rule

### Problem 4
Solve the system

$$
\begin{cases}
x + y + z = 4 \\
2x - y + z = 8 \\
x + 2y - z = 3
\end{cases}
$$

by Gaussian elimination. Then independently compute $z$ using Cramer's rule on the $3 \times 3$ coefficient matrix.

### Problem 5
Use the inverse-matrix method to solve

$$
\begin{cases}
3x + y = 11 \\
2x + y = 8
\end{cases}
$$

Compare your answer with direct subtraction.

### Problem 6
Classify each system and give its solution set when it exists:

(a)
$$
\begin{cases}
x + 2y = 3 \\
2x + 4y = 6
\end{cases}
$$

(b)
$$
\begin{cases}
x + 2y = 3 \\
2x + 4y = 7
\end{cases}
$$

## Section C: Parameter-dependent systems and homogeneous systems

### Problem 7
Discuss the number of solutions of

$$
\begin{cases}
x + y = 2 \\
x + ky = k + 1
\end{cases}
$$

for every real $k$, and solve whenever possible.

### Problem 8
Find all solutions of the homogeneous system

$$
\begin{cases}
x + 2y - z = 0 \\
2x + 4y - 2z = 0
\end{cases}
$$

Express the solution as a linear combination of two vectors.

### Problem 9
Solve the rectangular system

$$
\begin{cases}
x + y = 3 \\
2x - y = 0 \\
3x = 3
\end{cases}
$$

Explain why three equations in two unknowns need not be inconsistent.

---

## Section D: Applied linear system

### Problem 10
A workshop sells 30 tickets. A standard ticket costs 12 currency units and a student ticket costs 8. Revenue is 304. Form a linear system, solve it, and check that the answer is meaningful.
